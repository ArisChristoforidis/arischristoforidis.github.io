*Last Updated: October 9, 2025*

This Privacy Policy explains how Aristeidis Christoforidis (the "Developer," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the game Synoche (the "App") which is hosted on the Reddit platform. By interacting with the App's mechanisms on Reddit, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not engage with the App.
## 1. Information Processed

The Developer of Synoche is committed to minimizing data collection. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect typical Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or payment information.

### A. Public Data Processed for Game Functionality

The core function of Synoche relies on processing information that you have already made public on Reddit:

- __Reddit Username__: Your Reddit username is automatically processed when you intentionally submit a score or create a level by posting a comment or a thread title according to the game's rules. This is necessary to attribute scores and creations to you within the public context of the game.
- __User Content__: The content of the specific comments or posts used to interact with the game is processed to determine scores or define level parameters.
This data processing is visible and public on the Reddit platform itself, and is performed only to deliver the game features you requested.

### B. Automatically Processed Non-Personal Data

The App does not request access to device-specific permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, or Location). We may process the following non-personal data strictly for stability and compatibility, as provided by the underlying platforms (Reddit/hosting environment):
- __Usage & Stability Data__: Non-identifiable data used to monitor the App's performance, detect crashes, and ensure proper function (e.g., app version, error logs).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal information processed solely for the following purposes:
- __Game Functionality__: To record your score, create and store levels you submit, and ensure fair gameplay on Reddit.
- __Improvement__: To monitor the stability and performance of the game.

__We do not use any information for personalized advertising, marketing, or profiling.__

## 3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not share any personal information with third parties.
- __Public Processing__: All data relevant to the game (Reddit Username, scores, levels) is processed publicly on Reddit. By playing the game, you consent to this public display of your username and activity within the game threads/posts.
- __Legal Obligations__: We may disclose your information if legally required to do so in response to a valid court order or other governmental request.

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App is __not__ intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under 13. If you become aware of any data we have processed related to children under age 13, please contact us at the email below immediately.

## 5. Security of Your Information
Given the minimalist nature of data collection, the primary security measures involve respecting the privacy controls of the Reddit platform and ensuring our processing logic minimizes exposure to any non-public data.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights
Because we do not collect typical PII, the data we process is limited to public Reddit activity. If you wish to exercise your rights regarding this public data (e.g., requesting the removal of a score or level associated with your public username), please contact us directly using the email below. We will handle such requests on a case-by-case basis within the constraints of the Reddit platform's policies.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may modify this Privacy Policy as needed. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this Privacy Policy.
