Privacy Policy for Say 8

27 Jan 2026

Last Updated: January 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aristeidis Christoforidis (the "Developer," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the game Say 8 (the "App") which is hosted on the Reddit platform. By interacting with the App's mechanisms on Reddit, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not engage with the App.

1. Information Processed

The Developer of Say 8 is committed to minimizing data collection. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect typical Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or payment information.

A. Public Data & Game Functionality

The core function of Say 8 relies on processing information that you have already made public on Reddit:

  • Reddit Username: Your Reddit username is automatically processed when you intentionally submit a score or create a level by posting a comment or a thread title according to the game's rules. This is necessary to attribute scores and creations to you within the public context of the game.
  • User Content: Questions and answers for quizzes you create are stored and made public on Reddit.
  • Performance Data (Optional): Upon finishing a quiz, you may choose to have an automated app account post a comment on Reddit with your performance data. This is entirely optional and has no effect on gameplay.

B. Automatically Processed Data

The App does not request access to device-specific permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, or Location). We may process the following non-personal data strictly for stability and compatibility, as provided by the underlying platforms (Reddit/hosting environment):

  • Anonymized Scores: We collect anonymized scores to calculate rankings and game statistics.
  • Third-Party Processing: Quiz text is processed by the Google Gemini API for safety moderation. This data is handled according to Google's privacy standards for API processing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information solely to:

  • Operate game features (scoring, ranking, and quiz publishing).
  • Moderate content using AI tools to ensure a safe community.
  • Maintain the public leaderboard and attribution.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not share personal information with third parties for marketing.

  • Public Display: By playing, you consent to the public display of your username and activity (such as submitted quizzes) on the Reddit platform.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose your information if legally required to do so in response to a valid court order or other governmental request.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, consistent with Reddit’s User Agreement. We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under 13. If you become aware of any data we have processed related to children under age 13, please contact us at the email below immediately.

5. Security of Your Information

Given the minimalist nature of data collection, the primary security measures involve respecting the privacy controls of the Reddit platform and ensuring our processing logic minimizes exposure to any non-public data.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Because we do not collect typical PII, the data we process is limited to public Reddit activity. If you wish to exercise your rights regarding this public data (e.g., requesting the removal of a score or level associated with your public username), please contact us directly using the email below. We will handle such requests on a case-by-case basis within the constraints of the Reddit platform's policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may modify this Privacy Policy as needed. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this Privacy Policy.
